About Our Service

HI TECH SERVICE is an independent third-party repair provider serving customers in Mumbai. We handle service enquiries for LED, LCD and Smart televisions, including Sansui TVs.

Our repair process focuses on diagnosis first. Common enquiries include TVs that do not power on, blank or dim screens, backlight faults, sound problems, display lines, HDMI/input issues and Smart TV connectivity concerns.

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an authorized warranty service centre of Sansui. Brand names are used only to identify the type of product customers may request us to inspect.
