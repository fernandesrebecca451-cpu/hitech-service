Frequently Asked Questions

Useful Answers Before You Book

We keep the wording clear so customers know what can and cannot be confirmed before the appliance is inspected.

Do you repair all brands?

We provide independent multi-brand repair support for many commonly used appliance and TV brands. Service depends on the exact model, fault, condition and spare-part availability.

Can you confirm the final repair price over the phone?

Some basic jobs may be estimated, but the final repair amount usually depends on diagnosis and the parts required. An estimate should be discussed before repair work proceeds.

Is HI TECH SERVICE an authorized manufacturer service centre?

No manufacturer authorization is claimed on this website. HI TECH SERVICE is an independent third-party repair provider.

Do you guarantee same-day service?

No. A same-day visit may be possible in some cases, but it depends on technician availability, location, appliance type and booking time.

Can every appliance be repaired?

No. Some appliances may have unavailable parts, severe damage or repair costs that make repair impractical. Available options can be discussed after diagnosis.
